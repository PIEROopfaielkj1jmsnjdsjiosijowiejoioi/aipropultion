## Page 3 · Services / Offerings

**What we offer:**

1. **AI agents.** Custom AI that handles specific work — qualifying leads, organizing data, drafting things, running parts of your workflow.

2. **Custom software (your operating system).** A centralized web app that brings your tools and data into one place. Not just connecting APIs — the system you actually run your business from.

3. **Automation systems.** Repetitive tasks handed off to pipelines that run on their own.

4. **Workflow optimization.** We look at how work actually moves through your business, find what's broken, fix it — then automate what makes sense to automate.

5. **AI training.** Show your team how to use AI properly day-to-day.

6. **All of the above.** End-to-end AI-powered operations — agents, software, automation, training, all working together as one system.

_Most engagements combine two or three of these._
